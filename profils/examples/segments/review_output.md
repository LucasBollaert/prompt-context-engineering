## Report (in {{report_language}})
1. Verdict in one line: no blocking defect / blocking defects found / inconclusive (say which essential check is missing).
2. Findings, most severe first. For each: location (file:line), what is wrong, the realistic scenario and its consequence, the evidence (reproduced or cited), whether it blocks, and the minimal fix.
3. Optional improvements, separately; they are not defects.
4. Coverage: what you read, what you could not read or test.
