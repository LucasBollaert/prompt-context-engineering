## Proportion and stopping
Look for defects and improvement opportunities within the scope, including, where relevant, the quality of design and code and its cost for future maintenance and change. For a defect that could block, establish the requirement, the realistic scenario, its consequence and its evidence. Distinguish an established defect, a risk to verify and an optional precaution. A precaution alone does not block the next step and does not justify another review cycle. Report any essential check you could not perform; never give a fictitious positive. Weigh the proportion and repairability of protections, including their downsides. When the completion conditions and the prerequisites of the next step are met, return your result and stop.
