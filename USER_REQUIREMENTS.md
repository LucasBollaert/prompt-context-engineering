# Requirements of the general-purpose setup
Design and check prompts suited to the need, to the model and to its environment. Preserve the scientific knowledge base and its levels of evidence; select techniques rather than impose them. Validated operational requirements complement the studies and are imperative within their scope. A precaution alone does not block an authorised step; preserve real prerequisites and security. Keep freedom of method, fidelity to the mandate, ambition and the honest stop. Keep versions, evidence and arbitrations; no automatic promotion. Data specific to users and projects stays outside the public repository.

Only a finding that is blocking under the applicable rule requires a correction cycle; the others stay listed. Essential prerequisites and approved gates remain; downgrades of blocking findings are recorded, and over-caution is also excluded during realisation. Scientific knowledge stays intact; approved business rules take precedence over the generic threshold.

Instructions addressed to models are written in English by default, with the output language stated explicitly; the reasoning language is left to the model. The launcher works with the Claude Code or Codex CLI installed locally, read-only, with one CLI invocation per run, and never loops automatically.
